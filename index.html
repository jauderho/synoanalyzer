📥 Where to find synobackup.conf

The file location depends on your Synology DSM version:
DSM 7 / newer /var/packages/HyperBackup/etc/synobackup.conf
Older DSM /usr/syno/etc/synobackup.conf — or — /usr/syno/etc/packages/HyperBackup/synobackup.conf
Copy it off the NAS via SSH or File Station, then drop it here. All parsing happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
